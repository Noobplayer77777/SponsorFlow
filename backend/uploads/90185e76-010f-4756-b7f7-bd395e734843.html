Who We Are

ABOUT HACK CLUB

h.

Hack Club VIT Chennai is a student-run technology community built around one idea: nobody should have to learn to build alone. We bring together students who want to go beyond the classroom — through hackathons, workshops, and hands-on build sprints in AI/ML, web and app development, cybersecurity, and blockchain.

We're not a lecture series. Every event we run is built around shipping something real, in a room full of peers who are doing the same thing.

Code. Create. Collaborate.

What We Do

PAST EVENTS

h.

Our events go beyond tech, blending fun with learning — from treasure hunts and game nights to immersive offline experiences like paintball and HackQuest.

Flagship Hackathons
  • HackNight '25 — 36-hour offline hackathon · ₹1.25L prize pool · 5,000+ registrations
  • h.acKnight (2024) — 48-hour offline hackathon · ₹2L cash prize · 24×7 live support & mentorship
Technical & Competitive Events
  • CyberX — cybersecurity challenge in collaboration with Chennai Police
  • HackSecure — 10-hour cyber challenge
  • Valorant Tournament — competitive gaming · ₹33K prize pool
Community & Culture Events
  • Hack Sing Win 3.0 — a creative tech-music fusion, a Hack Club original
  • I Love Hackathon: Chennai Edition — $600 prize pool · WDC partnership
  • HACK-HER — 24-hour all-women hackathon · 200+ participants expected
  • VITBLOOM '25 — week-long campus-wide celebration of womanhood
  • BLOOM TANK — ideathon connecting student teams with funding & incubation
